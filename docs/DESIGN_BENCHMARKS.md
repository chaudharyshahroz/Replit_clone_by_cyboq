## Interim Reklu premium bar

Until the references are live, generated work is evaluated against observable product-design criteria:

- subject-specific information architecture rather than a universal SaaS sequence;
- coherent type hierarchy with one display, one body, and optional mono family;
- semantic color roles with verified contrast and controlled accent use;
- varied section rhythm instead of repeated equal-weight card grids;
- SVG icon consistency and imagery with a declared narrative purpose;
- meaningful first viewport and dominant primary action;
- intentional layouts at 1440, 1024, 768, 430, and 390 widths;
- restrained transform/opacity motion with reduced-motion support;
- originality without sacrificing comprehension or conversion.
